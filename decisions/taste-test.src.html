Recommendation

Let each reader's taste set their lines. My labels only set the starting point.

  1. Onboarding calibration, about two minutes. After someone writes their mutes, show 10 to 15 borderline tweets for those mutes, this same Keep / Hide card. Their answers set a personal line per mute. Without it every reader inherits my taste, and on dunking and AI hype my taste is the least reliable part of the system.
  2. Show clicks move the line afterwards. Each Show on a hide bar is a vote that the line is too low for that mute. Raise it a notch after a few, never lower it automatically. Lowering makes hides grow quietly and the reader can't see it.
  3. Treat "no line fits" as a product signal. If a reader's calls on a mute can't be matched by any threshold, their mute isn't something Jev's score tracks. Tell them so, and suggest a sharper rewording, instead of hiding at random.
  4. Labellers set defaults, not truth. The feed run's Opus labels should pick the default lines and nothing more. Your answers here are the first check on whether my labels are a fair default.

The one question I'd most want answered: how many wrongly hidden tweets a day would make you switch Signal & Noise off? That number sets every line. It decides how much noise we let through to stay under it.
