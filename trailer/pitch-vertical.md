# Signal & Noise — vertical trailer pitch

1080×1920, about 20 s, made for the X feed. The visual style stays the same: New York
serif, paper, watercolor washes, ink lines. What changes is the writing and the pacing.
The first cut read like an article (a label above each section, full sentences, axis
captions). This one is cut to music, has two to four words on screen at a time, and
lets the product do the explaining.

Music: about 120 BPM, so a beat every 0.5 s. Every cut lands on a beat. The track drops
out for the title.

Safe zones: nothing important in the bottom 20% (X's buttons) or the top 8% (status bar).
Text sits in the upper middle, 110 to 150 px.

The feed again. The three posts snap shut into thin bars: "Hidden · Political rage bait

**6. Bubbles (0:08.5 to 0:11)**
Bubbles drop in on the beat. Three get tapped, and each fills with paint on a beat.

A cursor types "Spoilers for Severance" and the pill fills blue.

The dot chart, cut down to its two rows: ordinary posts (blue) fall into a pile on the
left, noise (vermilion) on the right, and the ink line slams down between them on the
downbeat. No axis labels.
Text: **0 of 95 good posts hidden.**
Small line underneath: *test of 170 posts, three default mutes*

**9. Title (0:16 to 0:20)**
The music cuts out. The washes bloom behind "Signal" and "Noise".
Text: **Signal & Noise**
Under it: **For X. Runs on Jev.** (Alternative below.)

## Decisions for you

**Music.** Three options:
- **A (my pick):** I generate an original track with an open music model (MusicGen or
